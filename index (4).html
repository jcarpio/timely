HOW IT WORKS

From “this isn't working”
to “that's much better.”

No complicated forms. No endless spreadsheet juggling. Just your timetable, your priorities, and a helpful conversation.

01

Share what you have

Upload a photo or spreadsheet of your current timetable—or start with your subjects, teachers, and class groups. Review any details that need clarification.

02

Say what you need

“No PE after lunch.” “Keep Thursday afternoons free.” “Put lab sessions together.” Make requests in everyday language, not technical rules.

03

Review a better plan

Explore a proposed schedule, see exactly what changed, and refine it through follow-up requests. Conflicts and trade-offs are made visible.

REAL SCHOOL CHALLENGES

Scheduling that understands
your day-to-day reality.

From a single teacher's availability to a whole school's timetable, Timely helps turn difficult requests into workable plans.

📅

Teacher availability

Work around part-time contracts, shared staff, unavailable days, and personal scheduling constraints.

“Please keep Mr. Harris free every Friday.”
🔬

Double lessons & labs

Place practical sessions together and coordinate classes with specialized rooms or shared equipment.

“Biology needs two consecutive lessons in the lab.”
🏫

Classroom conflicts

Coordinate room availability across groups without accidentally assigning one space twice.

“Only one class can use the computer lab at a time.”
🧩

Last-minute changes

Rework plans when a teacher becomes unavailable or the school needs to adjust a lesson block.

“Can we move Friday's workshops to Wednesday?”
🎓

College departments

Coordinate multiple courses, instructors, shared lecture rooms, and teaching preferences.

“Avoid overlap between these two compulsory modules.”
↔️

Minimal disruption

Keep as much of the existing schedule as possible while adapting to new requirements.

“Change only what you absolutely need to.”
